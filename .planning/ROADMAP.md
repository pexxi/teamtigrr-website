# Roadmap: TeamTigrr Landing Page

## Overview

This roadmap transforms the TeamTigrr landing page from problem-focused messaging to benefit-driven communication. The journey starts with messaging and copy (the foundation that informs all content), then builds content sections (FAQ, social proof, how-it-works), and concludes with performance verification. Each phase delivers independently verifiable improvements.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Messaging & Copy
**Goal**: Hero and all copy communicate benefits positively, with privacy elevated as headline feature
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: COPY-01, COPY-02, COPY-03, COPY-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Hero headline communicates key benefit within 5 words
  2. No section uses problem-focused negative framing (no ban icons, no "chaos" language)
  3. Privacy protection appears in hero section or immediately below
  4. Swap system explanation shows "one request reaches all" benefit clearly
  5. Tone feels sporty and energetic (active verbs, positive language)
**Plans**: 2 plans in 2 waves

Plans:
- [x] 01-01-PLAN.md — Hero, problem section, and feature cards rewrite
- [x] 01-02-PLAN.md — Privacy section, swap explanation, CTAs, verification

**Goal**: Page performs well on mobile devices and loads fast
**Depends on**: Phase 2
