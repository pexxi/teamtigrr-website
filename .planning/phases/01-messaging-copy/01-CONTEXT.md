# Phase 1: Messaging & Copy - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-02
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Reframe all landing page copy from problem-focused to benefit-driven messaging. Privacy elevated as a headline feature. Sporty and energetic tone throughout. No new sections or features - this is copy rewrite only.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Hero headline & subtext
- Lead benefit: Effortless coordination ("one request, everyone responds")
- Audience framing: Speaks to the whole team collectively, not just leaders
- Emotional tone: Excitement - "this is going to be great" energy
- Subtext should name the category explicitly ("duty scheduling for sports teams")

### Privacy messaging
- Angle: Keep your contact info safe - general protection framing
- Placement: Dedicated section (not in hero, but prominent on page)
- Framing: Positive gain - "Your info stays yours" empowerment style
- Specificity: Keep it general, don't list what's not collected/shared

### Tone & voice
- Formality: Casual/friendly - like talking to a teammate, contractions OK
- Sports language: Light touch - occasional reference but not dominant
- CTAs: Action-focused ("Start now", "Try it free", "Get started")
- Pain points: Acknowledge briefly then pivot to solution - not purely positive

### Swap system explanation
- Key benefit: Fairness built in - "everyone gets equal chance to help"
- Explanation style: Focus on outcome, not the step-by-step flow
- Terminology: Use "swap" - it captures both parent-to-parent and volunteer scenarios
- Scope: Explicitly mention both uses - swap with another parent OR find a volunteer

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact headline wordsmithing (within 5-word constraint)
- Section ordering beyond privacy placement decision
- Specific verb choices and sentence structures
- How to balance excitement vs clarity

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- "Swap" is the right term because duties can be exchanged between parents, not just handed off to volunteers
- Hero should feel exciting, not relieved - positive energy over problem-solving relief

</specifics>
