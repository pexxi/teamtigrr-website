FAQ

Common questions.

Is TeamTigrr free?
Yes - completely free during our beta period. We're looking for teams to help us shape the product, and your feedback is the price of admission.
How long does setup take?
Under 10 minutes. Add your games, set up the duties each game needs, and invite your team's parents. The smart assistant handles the rest.
What about privacy?
Your data stays private. Contact details are only visible to the people who need them for their assigned duties. We don't sell data or create public volunteer lists.
Can we keep using our current tools alongside TeamTigrr?
Absolutely. TeamTigrr works alongside whatever you're already using. No need to switch everything at once - start with one team and see how it goes.
What sports are supported?
Currently basketball, football, and ice hockey. We're adding more sports based on demand - if yours isn't listed, let us know and we'll prioritize it.
